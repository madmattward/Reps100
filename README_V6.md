# Reps100 V6

V6 refines the blue UI, adds explicit sans-serif styling, preserves exercise-list scroll/search/filter state, improves exercise instructions, adds hip circumference, BMI/waist-to-height/waist-to-hip screening information, full-body measurement guides, streak calorie totals, DD/MM/YYYY history formatting, and chart layout fixes.

Health note: BMI and anthropometric ratios are screening measures. Reps100 does not diagnose disease or predict an individual heart attack or cardiovascular mortality risk.
