# Reps100 V35 – Trophy Cabinet

Forty image badges (10 categories × four tiers) are embedded as local drawable assets, cropped from the two generated badge sheets. Unlock dates persist in separate `v35StandardBadge_*` preferences, independently of the Centurian theme setting. Existing completed workout and Daily100 ledgers are used to backfill earned badges.

Categories and Bronze/Silver/Gold/Platinum thresholds:

- Strength: 500 / 2,500 / 10,000 / 25,000 resistance-equipment repetitions
- Endurance: 500 / 2,500 / 10,000 / 25,000 bodyweight repetitions
- Consistency: 3 / 7 / 30 / 100 distinct training dates
- Volume: 1,000 / 5,000 / 20,000 / 50,000 total repetitions
- Workout Dedication: 5 / 25 / 100 / 250 completed sessions (including Daily100)
- Exercise Variety: 5 / 10 / 20 / 40 different completed exercises
- Personal Best: 1 / 3 / 6 / 12 successive weekly repetition records (from stored history)
- Challenge Champion: 1 / 4 / 12 / 25 calendar weeks with at least three completed sessions (proxy for challenge consistency, not a claim of historic challenge completion)
- Calorie Milestones: 500 / 2,500 / 10,000 / 25,000 estimated kcal from saved workout records
- Daily100 Mastery: 5 / 20 / 50 / 100 Daily100 ledger completions

**Notes:** Existing V34 does not store historical personalised challenge awards or individual personal-best events. Accordingly V35 derives Challenge Champion from training weeks and Personal Best from weekly rep records; both descriptions explicitly explain this. Calorie milestones use saved workout calorie estimates; Daily100 calories are excluded rather than inventing values. Strength/endurance count exercises matching the catalog and categorise by equipment. A previously unlocked badge remains earned if workout history is later deleted. No Centurian progress is reset or modified.
